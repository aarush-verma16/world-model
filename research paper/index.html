The 14.5 gap (finding 20)

Hafner et al. 2023 Crafter XL is 14.5 ± 1.6 at 1M with train_ratio 512 and seq 64. Live M8-XL is ratio 32, seq 32, ~80k, held-out 1.36. Same metric, not the same experiment. Actor-critic graph is already finding-17-aligned; do not kill m8_xl_acfix2 to chase 14.5 in the same dir.

Why two weeks of extra losses could not work

The optimizer was solving a different problem than “train a DreamerV3 world model.”

Pre-reset graph

flowchart LR obs[obs 64x64] --> enc[encoder] enc --> emb[embedding] emb --> rssm[RSSM] rssm --> hz["feat h, z_post"] emb --> decE[embed decoder LIVE] hz --> decF["h,z decoder FROZEN"] decE --> pixE[recon_embed] decF --> pixH[recon_hz]

Pixel loss can fall on the left branch. The RSSM is optional.

Reset graph (what 50k trained)

flowchart LR obs[obs 64x64] --> enc[encoder] enc --> rssm[RSSM] rssm --> feat["feat = concat h, z_post"] feat --> dec[one live decoder] feat --> rew[symlog two-hot reward] feat --> cont[continue BCE] rssm --> kl[KL post || prior] dec --> pix[sum-MSE image]

No path reconstructs without z_posterior.
